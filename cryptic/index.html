What’s a cryptic clue?

Every cryptic clue is a tiny puzzle with two parts:

  • a definition — a direct hint at the answer.
  • and wordplay — a clever way to build the answer using some mix of letterplay and language tricks.

What’s a cryptic clue?

The wordplay usually has two ingredients:

  • Fodder — the letters and words you’ll be messing with.
  • Indicators — words that tell you what to do with the fodder (mix it up, reverse it, take initials…)

How do you play?

Type your answer and hit check. Stuck? Tap hints and pick one — each lights up its part of the clue (definition, indicator, fodder) and counts as a stroke, like golf.

Using hints is how we learn — it’s always okay to lean on them.
